Case Studies

Selected Engineering Projects

Real-world production problem-solving, poka-yoke implementation, material flow optimization, and cost saving initiatives.

PROJECT 01

Transmission Assembly Poka-Yoke Improvements

Challenge Risk of human assembly errors in transmission components during shop floor operations.
Improvement Designed and implemented 2 dedicated poka-yoke mechanisms specifically for the Spline shaft and PTO drive shaft.
Result Error prevention and continuous assembly improvement on the transmission line.
PROJECT 02 High Impact Achievement

₹4 Lakh Cost-Saving Initiative

Challenge High scrap and replacement costs resulting from rejected ratchet torque tooling components.
Improvement Engineered a modification by fixing rejected ratchet torque using socket adaptors to restore complete operational tooling capability.
Result Saved ₹4 lakh in tooling expenditure and recovered assembly efficiency without compromising quality.
PROJECT 03

QRC Coupling Implementation

Challenge Inefficiencies and spill risks during transmission oil draining processes.
Improvement Implemented Quick Release Coupling (QRC) system for transmission oil draining operations.
Result Improved draining cycle time, shop-floor cleanliness, and overall operational efficiency.
PROJECT 04

Kitting Trolley Modification

Challenge Material handling constraints and storage alignment for counter gearing components.
Improvement Modified 3 specialized kitting trolleys tailored specifically for counter gearing material presentation.
Result Optimized parts accessibility, enhanced line ergonomics, and secured material integrity.
