PROJECT 01 ₹4.2 Lakh Cost Saved

Cost Saving Project

Mahindra (Offsite) Apr 2021 – May 2021 (Full Time)
Problem Statement

During Transmission assembly, various types of torque wrenches were used. During continuous operational use, the torque wrench heads became damaged and worn, causing production downtime and recurring tool replacement expenses.

Engineering Solution

Welded and permanently fixed a reducer onto the head of the torque wrench head. This robust modification permanently resolved the head failure issue with zero defects or operational problems during active use on the production line.

Financial & Operational Impact

Successfully saved company costs of ₹4.2 Lakh while eliminating line interruptions and recurring tooling replacement expenses.

PROJECT 02 Poka-Yoke Zero Defect

H1 Poka-Yoke Tooling Design

Mahindra (Offsite) Dec 2020 – Dec 2020 (Full Time)
Problem Statement

In the H1 tractor model, the spline shaft circlip was found missing after 4th gear during quality gate audits, posing a critical transmission sub-assembly defect risk.

Engineering Solution

Designed a specialized Poka-Yoke tool with a spring-loaded stud mechanism that moves up and down to touch and activate a sensor switch at the bottom. Once validated, the sub-assembly conveyor stopper lowers, allowing the entire final gearing assembly to proceed to the tilter, ready for mounting onto the main conveyor.

Quality & Operational Impact

Guaranteed 100% automated defect prevention and zero missing circlip escapes, ensuring flawless quality compliance at gate audits and automated line conveyor sequencing.
